# Real receipt samples: CORD-v2

These three real Indonesian receipt photographs are from the **training split** of CORD-v2 by Park et al., Clova AI/NAVER. They are not generated invoices or held-out test examples.

Source: https://github.com/clovaai/cord and https://huggingface.co/datasets/naver-clova-ix/cord-v2 . License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). See the included [license text](LICENSE-CC-BY.txt).

Images are the publisher dataset viewer's JPEG derivatives, retained without further modification. Original index, image SHA-256, currency and mapped published values are in [catalog.json](catalog.json). CORD's released annotations omit some store/payment information; missing labels must not be fabricated. Values shown for comparison are not passed to extraction. Dates, layout and IDR amounts differ from Indian GST invoices.
